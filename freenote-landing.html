Frequently Asked Questions

Is this for notes or tasks? +
It's primarily a notes-first application, but with a fully integrated, powerful task engine underneath. You write notes normally, but any inline action items you create are automatically syndicated to a global task manager.
Can I organize work by project/activity? +
Yes! Freenote introduces the concept of "Activities." You group related notes and tasks into Activities (like "Website Redesign" or "Q3 Planning"), keeping everything cleanly siloed but easily accessible.
Can I see all my tasks in one place? +
Absolutely. While tasks live inside their origin notes, Freenote aggregates them into a global "Activities" sidebar where you can view every pending task across all your notes at once.
Who is freenote best for? +
It's built for founders, product managers, researchers, writers, and anyone whose tasks usually require a paragraph of context. If you find yourself pasting links to Google Docs inside your Asana or Todoist tasks, Freenote is for you.
