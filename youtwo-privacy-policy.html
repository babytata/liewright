Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

At YouTwo, your privacy is our priority. We believe that your notes are your personal space. This policy outlines our approach to data collection and privacy for the YouTwo iOS app and our website.

1. Data Collection in the YouTwo App

The YouTwo app is designed to be a private, local-first note-taking experience.

  • We do not collect personal information. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we track your notes, ideas, or personal usage within the app.
  • Your data stays with you. All notes created in YouTwo are saved locally on your device.
  • iCloud Syncing. If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your notes are synced across your devices using Apple's iCloud services. This data is managed directly by Apple, and YouTwo does not have access to it or store it on any third-party servers. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for more details on how iCloud secures your data.

2. Data Collection on the YouTwo Website

Our website (where you download or learn about the app) may collect some basic data to help us improve the user experience:

  • Analytics: We may use basic, anonymized analytics to understand how people find and navigate our website. This does not include any information that can personally identify you.
  • Cookies: Our website may use cookies to ensure it functions properly and to improve your browsing experience.

3. Third-Party Services

The YouTwo app does not use third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs. The app is completely ad-free and tracking-free.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we ever decide to change our privacy policy in the future (for example, if we introduce new features that require a different approach), we will update this page. Since we don't hold your email address, we cannot notify you directly, so we encourage you to review this page periodically.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us via our support channels.
