Pricing FAQ

Is it really a one-time purchase? +
Yes. Freenote Pro is a one-time purchase. You buy the app, and it's yours. We believe productivity software shouldn't tax you monthly just to write notes. The license includes all point-updates and a full year of dedicated support.
Are there team plans available? +
Freenote is currently designed for solo operators and individual contributors to manage their personal context. We are exploring team collaboration features for the future, but right now, the focus is on the single-player experience.
What happens during the Beta? +
The beta is free to use while we gather feedback and squash bugs. Beta users will receive a significant discount if they choose to upgrade to the Pro License when version 1.0 officially launches.
