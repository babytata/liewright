✨ Powerful Features

Built for Your Workflow

Every feature designed to make dual-pane note-taking seamless, powerful, and delightful.

Lightning Fast

Native iOS performance with 120Hz scrolling. No lag, no compromises. Built with SwiftUI for buttery-smooth interactions.

Adjustable Split

Drag the divider to your perfect ratio. Snaps to 33%, 50%, or 67%. Or focus on one pane at a time—your workspace, your rules.

Auto-Save Everything

Never lose a thought. Every keystroke is automatically saved. Your notes persist across sessions, devices, and dreams.

iCloud Sync

Seamlessly sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Start on one device, continue on another. Your notes follow you everywhere.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Power-user friendly with Mac Catalyst support. Create notes, switch panes, and navigate—all without lifting your hands.

Dark Mode Native

Beautiful in light, stunning in dark. Automatically adapts to your system preferences for comfortable writing at any hour.
